Google Sheets Not Connected

Sync your Collect Sync inventory with a Google Sheet. Any changes you make in the sheet will update your inventory, and vice versa. Great for managing inventory on the go or sharing with your team.

No sheet connected

How to Connect

1

Create a Google Sheet

Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet. You can name it anything you like (e.g. "My Card Inventory").

2

Share it with Collect Sync

Click the Share button in your Google Sheet, then add this email address as an Editor:

card-sync-sheets@card-sync-491400.iam.gserviceaccount.com

Make sure to select "Editor" (not Viewer) so Collect Sync can write to your sheet.

3

Paste your Sheet URL below

Copy the full URL from your browser's address bar and paste it into the connection form below. It looks like:
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/abc123.../edit

4

Connect and sync

Click Connect Sheet and Collect Sync will verify access. Then use the sync buttons to push your inventory to the sheet or pull changes back. Your sheet headers will be set up automatically.

eBay Not Connected

List your Collect Sync inventory on eBay automatically. Push your cards as eBay listings and keep stock levels in sync across platforms. Requires Business plan or higher.

Not connected

How to Connect

1

Click "Connect eBay"

You'll be redirected to eBay's login page to authorize Collect Sync to manage your listings.

2

Log in and approve

Sign into your eBay seller account and click "Agree" to grant Collect Sync access to your inventory and listings.

3

Start syncing

Once connected, use the sync buttons to push your inventory to eBay as live listings. Collect Sync handles the rest.
